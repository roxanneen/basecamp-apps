Basecamp apps by thehat

Unofficial hobby apps for Logos Basecamp. Add the catalogue once, then install and update them from inside Basecamp.

Not official. These are personal, experimental hobby projects. They are not Logos products, they have not been audited, and they come with no warranty. They are not affiliated with or endorsed by Logos or Basecamp.

Add the catalogue to Basecamp

https://raw.githubusercontent.com/roxanneen/basecamp-apps/main/logos-repo.json
  1. Open Basecamp and click Applications in the menu on the right.
  2. Click Manage Repositories.
  3. Paste the address into Add a repository and click Add.
  4. Go back to Applications and scroll down to Basecamp apps by thehat. Click an app to install it.

Works with Basecamp 0.3.2 or newer on macOS with an Apple chip and on 64-bit Linux desktops. Not on Windows, Intel Macs, phones or tablets. New to Basecamp? Follow the step-by-step guide, or download Basecamp.

What adding it means

  • You are trusting the publisher. Every Basecamp app runs inside your Basecamp session with no sandbox. Adding a catalogue means trusting whoever signs its packages.
  • The packages are signed. Each one carries a signature made with the publisher key below. Basecamp checks it, and the file's hash, before installing.
  • The signature says who published the file. It does not say the app is safe or reviewed. Each app's code is in its own repository, linked on its card.
  • This catalogue lists only these apps. It never lists a package from the official Logos catalogue.
